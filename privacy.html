Privacy policy – Akuru Studio

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Short version: nothing you type or create ever leaves your device. There is no account, no sign-in, no cookies, no ads and no server that receives your pictures or your text. The only thing ever counted is anonymous: visits to the website pages, a 👍 or 👎 if you choose to tap one in the web app, and that the web app was installed. The Chrome extension and the Android app from Google Play send nothing at all.

What I collect

Akuru Studio has no backend. Your photos, your Sinhala text, your designs and your settings never leave your device.

  • No personal information is collected or transmitted.
  • No telemetry, crash reporting or tracking of what you do in the editor.
  • No cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or advertising identifiers.
  • No account, e-mail address or sign-in of any kind.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, because nothing is collected.

What stays on your device

Everything you do happens inside your browser, on your own machine. The editor saves a few conveniences so the app feels the same next time you open it:

  • Browser local storage (keys starting with akuru-): your saved text styles, "My templates", photo-effect presets, quick phrases, brush settings, which help sections are folded open, print settings, recently used emoji and skin tone.
  • IndexedDB (database akuru-studio): any font files you add via "My fonts", any sticker images you add via "My stickers", and an automatic copy of your last design (its texts, photos and a small preview) so you can continue where you left off after closing the tab.
  • App offline copy (web version installed as an app only): your browser keeps a copy of the Akuru Studio page, its icons and the Google Fonts files so the app opens without internet. It contains no designs or personal data.

Only one design is kept this way, and it never leaves your device. Use 📁 Project ▸ Save project file to keep a design as a file of your own.

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing your browser's site data for Akuru Studio. Nothing is kept anywhere else, so that erases it completely.

Files you open and save

Photos, stickers and project files you add are read directly by your browser. They are never uploaded. When you save a PNG, JPG, PDF or project file, your browser writes the file to your own device.

Fonts and network access

  • Chrome extension: fully offline. All fonts are bundled inside the extension, so it makes no network requests at all.
  • Web version: loads font files from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com). That is the only network request the page makes. Google may log the IP address and request details for those font requests under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). If you prefer zero third-party requests, use the Chrome extension instead.
  • Links and sharing: the "Get more at openmoji.org" button, the "Visual guide" link and the 💌 Share buttons (WhatsApp, Facebook) only open those websites when you click them; ✉️ Send feedback only opens your own email app. Akuru Studio itself sends nothing there.

Anonymous counts (website, 👍 / 👎 and installs)

To know whether people find Akuru Studio useful, three things are counted with GoatCounter (https://www.goatcounter.com/privacy), a privacy-friendly service that uses no cookies, does not store IP addresses and shares nothing with third parties:

  • Website visits – each time someone opens the landing page, the guide or this privacy page on adurage.github.io. GoatCounter records only aggregate numbers: the page, the site you came from, browser, screen size and country.
  • 👍 / 👎 in the web app – only if you tap one, a single anonymous "vote-up" or "vote-down" is counted. Nothing you typed or made is sent. Your choice is remembered in your browser so it is counted once.
  • Installing the app – when you install the web app on your phone or computer, one anonymous "installed" is counted, nothing else.

Opening or using the editor itself is not counted. The Chrome extension never counts anything and makes no network requests. The Android app on Google Play opens the same web app but counts nothing (no 👍/👎, no install count); like the web version it loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Chrome extension permissions

The extension requests no host permissions and no access to your browsing data. It only opens its own editor page when you click the toolbar icon.

Children

Akuru Studio is suitable for all ages. Since no data is collected, nothing can be collected from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top will change with it. Because the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification.
