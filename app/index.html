    🚀 Quick start

    1. Add a photo – or pick a colour / template.
    2. Type in Singlish – ammaa becomes අම්මා on the picture.
    3. Tap a one-tap style to make it beautiful.
    4. Save as PNG/JPG, or print as PDF.

    💾 Save your project

    • 📁 Project ▸ Save project file keeps your whole design – texts, photos, stickers, drawings – in one .akuru.json file. Open it later, on any device, and keep editing. Shortcut: Ctrl+S.
    • Your last design is kept in this browser. When you come back, tap Continue to pick up where you left off.
    • Fonts you added yourself are not stored in the file. Add the same font on the other device – until then a fallback font is shown.

    ✍️ Typing Sinhala

    • Type English letters the way you say the word: suba upandhinayak → සුබ උපන්දිනයක්.
    • Double a vowel to make it long: a → අ, aa → ආ, ee → ඒ. x gives ං (sixhala → සිංහල).
    • Keep English words with braces: {Happy} birthday.
    • "As typed" switches off conversion – use it with a Sinhala keyboard (e.g. Helakuru).
    • 📋 Copy Sinhala text puts the Sinhala on your clipboard for SMS / Facebook.
    • ➕ New text adds another separate text block.
    • 🤏 Zoom: on a phone, put two fingers on the picture and spread them to zoom in (pinch to zoom out); while zoomed, drag an empty spot to look around. On a computer: Ctrl + mouse wheel or pinch on the trackpad. Tap ↺ (top right) to go back. Zoom only changes the view – your saved picture is not affected.
    • 🔍 Expand (under the picture) makes the picture fill the screen – easier to move and resize text on a phone. Works upright and sideways; tap ✓ Done (or Esc) to go back.

    🎨 Styling text

    • One-tap styles: 32 ready looks – tap one with your text selected.
    • My styles: style a text, type a name, 💾 Save style. Up to 30. Tick "Include font" to save the font too.
    • Fine-tune with Size, Colour, Outline, Shadow / glow and Background box (folded sections).
    • Styles scale to the text size – the same style works on big titles and small lines.

    🔤 Fonts

    • 14 built-in fonts – 6 Sinhala, 8 English.
    • Add your own: ＋ Add font files (.ttf, .otf, .woff). They stay saved in this browser.
    • Old FM / DL / KD fonts (from font.akasa.lk) are detected and converted automatically – keep typing Singlish.
    • If a font looks like gibberish, change its type in the dropdown under the font (Unicode / Legacy FM / Kaputa).
    • Installed fonts: type the name (e.g. Iskoola Pota) and tap Add.

    📐 Canvas, sizes & templates

    • Post templates: one tap sets size, background and text style. Your words stay.
    • My templates: design a look you like, name it and tap 💾 Save as my template. It keeps the background, frame, effect, text style, font and size – not your words or photos – so you can reuse it in one tap.
    • Social media: exact sizes for Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, YouTube, TikTok and more.
    • Aspect ratio + Resolution: e.g. 9:16 at 1080p = 1080×1920. Use 4K or Print sizes for printing.
    • Background: Solid, Gradient (2–3 colours, linear/radial), Pattern (12 kinds), Photo or Transparent – save as PNG to keep a see-through background (logos, stickers).

    🖼️ Photos

    • Choose a background photo, drag & drop it on the canvas, or paste it (Ctrl+V).
    • "Resize canvas to the photo's shape" keeps the whole photo. Fill / Whole photo changes cropping.
    • Photo on photo: add a second picture, then make it square, rounded or a circle with a border.

    💌 Share & feedback

    • ✉️ Send feedback opens an email to us – tell us what you like or what to improve.
    • 👍 / 👎 tells us if you like it. Only your tap is counted, anonymously – nothing you type or make.

    😊 Emoji & stickers

    • All phone emoji in categories, with search, skin tones and Recently used.
    • "This device" looks like your phone; "Google" looks the same everywhere.
    • My stickers: add your own PNG or SVG files – for example free emoji from openmoji.org – and use them with one tap. On a phone, SVG files are in your Files app (Downloads), not the gallery – tap 📁 From Files. Credit "OpenMoji – CC BY-SA 4.0" when you share.

    ✏️ Drawing & shapes

    • Pick a brush – drawing turns on. Tap Done (under the picture) to move things again.
    • 10 brushes: pen, pencil, paint brush, dry brush (real paint strokes with bristles and splashes – great behind text), highlighter, calligraphy, airbrush, crayon, neon, eraser.
    • 14 shapes – drag on the picture. Hold Shift for straight lines and even shapes. Fill + separate fill colour.
    • Size, Opacity and Smoothing (steadier lines). Stylus pressure works on tablets.
    • 💧 Pick from picture copies a colour. The eraser never touches your photo or text.

    ✨ Photo effects

    • 25 effects with live previews of your photo. Strength makes any effect softer or stronger.
    • 16 sliders in Light, Colour and Detail & texture.
    • My effects: adjust, name it, 💾 Save effect. Up to 30.

    🔲 Frames

    • 27 frames: classic, shape (circle, arch, heart), fun and neon.
    • Colour style: Solid, Gradient or Rainbow. Glow controls neon strength.

    👆 Moving & layers

    • Tap to select. Drag to move – pink lines show the centre.
    • Orange corner square = resize. White dot on top = rotate.
    • Double-tap text to edit it. Buttons under the picture: Copy, Center, Front, Back, Delete.
    • Layers tab lists everything – reorder or delete there.

    ⬇ Saving & printing

    • ⬇ Save → PNG (best quality) or JPG (smaller file).
    • 🖨 Print → choose paper (A4, A3…), then Download PDF or Print now.
    • ⚠️ "May look blurry"? Tap "Make my canvas this paper size" or raise Resolution.
    • Inside the Claude viewer, direct printing is blocked – download the PDF and print it.

    💾 What is saved, and where

    • Saved automatically in this browser: your fonts, styles, effects, quick phrases, brushes, folded sections and print settings.
    • Not saved: the picture you are working on – save it as PNG before closing.
    • ⬇ Export / ⬆ Import (styles and effects) to back up, move to another device or share.
    • Clearing browser data removes saved items – export now and then.

    🛟 Problems & fixes

    • Sinhala shows as boxes or wrong letters – pick a Sinhala font, or set your added font's type to Legacy FM.
    • Text too big after changing size – drag the corner square, or use the Size slider.
    • Can't move text – drawing is on. Tap Done under the picture.
    • Print looks soft – use High (300 DPI) and "Make my canvas this paper size".
    • Fonts look plain offline – the web version loads fonts from the internet; the Chrome extension has them built in.
